# CombustionLens product review

## Demonstration story

Lead with a question: can a result from a microgravity droplet experiment be transferred to solid materials in a lunar habitat?

1. Open the evidence explorer. Switch the conceptual flame illustration between gravity regimes. Identify it as a teaching illustration.
2. Prioritize Detection & extinction. Read the FLEX observation, its interpretation and its limitation. Open the original NASA source.
3. Choose Lunar surface. Open the physical boundary checks. Inspect the gravity mismatch and the fuel mismatch for FLEX.
4. In “What would close the gap?”, select FLEX. Show that the research plan calls for matching primary research and run-level records, rather than claiming a safe condition.
5. Export the applicability assessment. Return to the explorer, add the selected observation to a brief, and export the source-linked brief.

## Product distinction

The central contribution is an explicit evidence-transfer workflow. Observations, interpretations, mismatches and missing conditions remain separate. The categorical coverage strip describes six boundary checks; it is not a probability, confidence estimate or risk model.

The workspace uses a responsive research navigation, navy surfaces, copper actions and teal evidence annotations. Existing retrieval, comparison and synthesis routes remain accessible. Those routes require a running backend and populated corpus; the curated explorer and boundary checks work without a model.
